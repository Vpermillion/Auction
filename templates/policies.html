PLATFORM POLICIES / DRAFT FOR MONGOLIAN LEGAL REVIEW

Terms and privacy

This preview uses synthetic shops, accounts and inventory. It is not approved for real-money trading.

How the pilot works

Ordinary ascending-price timed auctions use free bidding. There are no purchased bids, deposits, wallets, escrow or automatic penalties. The published starting price is the minimum acceptable sale price. Bid increments, fees, deadline, soft close and payment window appear on each lot before bidding. The server accepts a valid bid only before the deadline. Highest accepted bid wins. With no bids, no sale is awarded.

Seller and platform responsibilities

The seller handles inventory, ownership and authenticity claims, condition, certificates, payment arrangements, fulfillment and customer remedies. The platform supplies software, bidding records and technical support and remains responsible for its own actions, privacy and software operation. Seller identity checks are separate from item inspection or authentication. No generic verification badge is used.

Corrections, cancellations and nonpayment

All material terms freeze on publication. Before closing, a seller can cancel with a reason, retaining all accepted bids. Corrections require a new draft and auction. Operator suspension stops an open listing without a winner, preserving history. Stopped listings cannot resume. Closed auctions retain the recorded outcome, even if the buyer or shop is later suspended. Concerns about a closed outcome require operator review through a report.

A winner's payment deadline is measured from the final extended closing time. After that deadline, a seller can record unpaid review and document a relisting decision. The original outcome remains visible. There is no automatic charge, penalty or reassignment to underbidders. Payment is recorded only as seller-confirmed, never as provider-verified. No actual payment should be sent in this preview.

Concerns and disputes

Contact the shop using its published contact for item condition, payment or fulfillment issues. A signed-in user can submit a private report on any published auction. The operator can review reports and suspend accounts, shops or listings with recorded reasons. In this local preview, reports are stored for manual review; there are no email notifications or promised response times. These tools cannot reliably detect related accounts, collusion or all shill bidding.

Privacy and retention draft

We store an email, a password hash, an assigned public alias, bids, reports and audit records. We do not request identity documents, real names, phone numbers or payment card data. Public histories use aliases only. A winning buyer's email is available to the seller for their own shop's award. Operators can access internal user records and reports. Basic request rate limits use a short-lived keyed hash of the connection IP, not a stored raw IP.

Only submit synthetic information in this preview. Data is kept in the local SQLite database until the operator removes that demo database. Before collecting real information, agree a lawful retention and deletion schedule, a privacy contact and process for access or correction requests, secure backups and incident handling with local counsel. Accepted bids and audit events are append-only through this application; a database administrator can still change the database outside it.

Language and legal review

English and Mongolian interface labels are available. Longer policy, operator and seller-provided text remains in English and needs professional Mongolian translation and review. This draft makes no claim of Mongolian legal approval or guaranteed authenticity. Pricing, seller agreements, buyer eligibility and contract enforceability remain unresolved for launch.
